Rooms (feet)

About this

Area = length × width; add the waste allowance, divide by box coverage and round up to whole boxes. Waste percentages are common rules of thumb (5 to 15%), not a standard; check your product's spec sheet for its coverage and your installer's advice for tricky layouts. Buying one box more than the minimum is common, because later batches can differ in colour. Worked example matches tradesppl.com: 12 × 15 ft, 10% waste, 20 sq ft boxes gives 10 boxes. About
